**Artist:** Big Bill Broonzy / Derek & the Dominos
**Year:** 1940 (Broonzy) / 1970 (Derek & the Dominos)
**Style:** Slow blues
**Tempo:** Slow (~75 BPM)

- 8-bar blues form (shorter than standard 12-bar)
- Verses over slow groove
- Guitar solo
- Extended jam possible (Derek & the Dominos version)

## Notes
- Classic "leaving town" blues — "I'm gonna walk on"
- 8-bar form, not 12-bar — shorter cycle
- Derek & the Dominos (Layla album) did the famous rock version
- Slow, soulful, great for showing feeling
- Perfect for late-set when you want to cool things down
- Great guitar showcase — emotional soloing over simple changes
- Good for senior audiences — gentle, familiar
- **Vocal starts on the 1** — "Gonna" hits on beat 1 of bar 1
