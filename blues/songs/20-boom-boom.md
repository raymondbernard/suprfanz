# Boom Boom

**Key:** E
**Artist:** John Lee Hooker
**Year:** 1962
**Style:** Boogie blues
**Tempo:** Medium groove (~95 BPM)

## YouTube
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E09t5mJECqg

## Structure
- One-chord boogie vamp (E throughout)
- Verse/chorus sections
- Guitar solo
- Stop-time breaks

## Chord Progression
- Main vamp: E7 throughout (boogie pattern)
- Occasional: A7 — E7 — B7 — E7 (turnaround)

## Notes
- John Lee Hooker's biggest hit — "Boom boom boom boom"
- Boogie pattern — not standard 12-bar, more of a vamp
- Call and response: "Boom boom" → guitar answer
- Great audience participation — "boom boom" is fun to shout
- The groove IS the song — hypnotic, driving
- Perfect for getting people to dance
- Simple but unstoppable energy
- **Vocal starts on the 1** — "Boom" hits on beat 1 of bar 1
