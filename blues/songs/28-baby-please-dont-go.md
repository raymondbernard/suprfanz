## Notes
- Delta blues classic — Muddy Waters made it electric
- Them (Van Morrison) made it a rock song — much heavier
- F# key is unusual — gives it a distinctive color
- Stop-time figure — dramatic pauses, then back to the groove
- Great for building tension and release
- The "baby please don't go" plea — emotional, desperate
- Good mid-set tension builder
