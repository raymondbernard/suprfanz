# Mannish Boy

**Key:** A
**Artist:** Muddy Waters
**Year:** 1955
**Style:** Chicago blues, one-chord groove
**Tempo:** Slow-medium (~80 BPM)

## YouTube
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zVp3C_Kro0A

## Structure
- One-chord vamp on A (with occasional IV to V movement)
- Call-and-response vocal pattern
- Harmonica solo
- Guitar solo

## Chord Progression
- Main vamp: A7 throughout
- Occasional move: D7 — A7 — E7 — A7 (turnaround)

## Notes
- "I'm a man" — classic male swagger blues
- Built on a single chord vamp, not full 12-bar
- Call and response between vocal and instrument
- Muddy Waters' answer to Bo Diddley's "I'm a Man"
- Great for building energy and audience participation
- The "Yeah yeah yeah yeah" parts are perfect for sing-along
- **Vocal starts on the 1** — "Yeah" hits on beat 1 of bar 1
