# Red House

**Key:** Bb
**Artist:** Jimi Hendrix
**Year:** 1967 (Are You Experienced)
**Style:** Slow blues
**Tempo:** Slow (~75 BPM)

## YouTube
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5sbS2u9GXIA

## Structure
- 12-bar blues × verses
- Extended guitar solo (2-3 choruses)
- Return to verse
- Outro

## Chord Progression (12-bar blues in Bb)
| Bar | 1-4 | 5-6 | 7-8 | 9-10 | 11-12 |
|-----|------|-----|------|-------|--------|
| Chord | Bb7 | Eb7 | Bb7 | F7 | Bb7 — F7 |

## Notes
- Hendrix's blues masterpiece — slow, soulful
- Guitar tone is everything here — warm, fat, sustained
- Bending and vibrato are key techniques
- Great for showing emotional playing, not just speed
- Extended solo section is a showcase
- One of the best slow blues ever recorded
