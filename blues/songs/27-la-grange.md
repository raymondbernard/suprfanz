# La Grange

**Key:** A
**Artist:** ZZ Top
**Year:** 1973
**Style:** Texas blues boogie
**Tempo:** Medium boogie (~120 BPM)

## Structure
- Intro riff (repeated)
- Verse over riff
- Guitar solo
- Outro

## Signature Riff
```
A:  A  C  E  A  C  E  (boogie pattern, repeated)
    Shuffle feel — swing 8th notes
```

## Notes
- ZZ Top's signature — "that hutch" of a song
- Inspired by John Lee Hooker's "Boogie Chillen"
- The riff is the whole song — boogie pattern in A
- Shuffle feel — relaxed, greasy
- Great guitar solo showcase
- Perfect energy raiser — crowds love it
