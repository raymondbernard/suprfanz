## Notes
- Willie Dixon wrote it, Howlin' Wolf recorded it, Cream made it epic
- "A spoonful" — suggestive, playful, classic blues double meaning
- Cream version is heavier, more extended — great guitar showcase
- The riff drives the song — simple, powerful
- Good for building energy gradually
- Clapton's tone and phrasing on the Cream version are masterclass
