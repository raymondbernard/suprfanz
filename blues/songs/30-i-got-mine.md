# I Got Mine

**Key:** B
**Artist:** The Black Keys
**Year:** 2008
**Style:** Blues-rock / garage blues
**Tempo:** Medium (~110 BPM)

## YouTube
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DZa-EXC7ST4

## Structure
- Riff-based verse
- Chorus
- Guitar solo
- Bridge/breakdown

## Chord Progression
- Main riff: B → E → B → F# (variations)
- Roughly blues-based but not standard 12-bar

## Notes
- The Black Keys — modern blues-rock
- Dan Auerbach brings blues into the 21st century
- Heavy, fuzzed-out guitar tone
- Good for showing blues is alive and evolving
- Younger audiences recognize this immediately
- Great energy number — driving, raw
- Pair with Bad to the Bone for a power finale section
