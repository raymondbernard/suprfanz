## Notes
- Written by John D. Loudermilk, made famous by Nashville Teens (1964)
- Blues-rock energy — raw, driving
- Great storytelling — "born in a trunk, raised in a ditch"
- Edgar Winter and Steve Earle also covered it
- Good change of pace — not the usual Chicago blues
- Energy raiser — gets rooms moving
