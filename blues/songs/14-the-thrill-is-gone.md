# The Thrill Is Gone

**Key:** Gb
**Artist:** B.B. King
**Year:** 1969
**Style:** Slow blues
**Tempo:** Slow (~70 BPM)

## YouTube
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oica5jG7FpU

## Structure
- 12-bar blues × verses
- Guitar solo (signature vibrato)
- String pad accompaniment in original

## Notes
- B.B. King's signature song — won a Grammy, made him a star
- Minor key blues — moodier than standard blues
- The vibrato on the guitar is THE thing to nail
- String section on the original (can approximate with guitar pad)
- Perfect for senior audiences — everyone knows this song
- Emotional, dramatic, the ultimate "blues is feeling sad" song
- Gb is an unusual key — gives it a distinctive, dark color
- Can capo up or play in F if Gb is uncomfortable
