# Born Under a Bad Sign

**Key:** C#
**Artist:** Albert King
**Year:** 1967
**Style:** Soul-blues
**Tempo:** Medium slow (~80 BPM)

## YouTube
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2Py37G9qsfY

## Structure
- 12-bar blues × verses
- Guitar solo
- Horn-like fills between vocal lines

## Chord Progression (12-bar blues in C#)
| Bar | 1-4 | 5-6 | 7-8 | 9-10 | 11-12 |
|-----|------|-----|------|-------|--------|
| Chord | C#7 | F#7 | C#7 | G#7 | C#7 — G#7 |

## Notes
- Albert King's signature — the "bad luck" blues
- Written by Booker T. Jones and William Bell (Stax Records)
- Cream covered it — Clapton worshipped Albert King
- Soul-blues feel — slightly different from Chicago blues
- Bending notes are key — Albert King played left-handed (strings upside down)
- Great storytelling lyrics — "born under a bad sign, I been down since I began to crawl"
- Perfect mid-set tempo change
