# Alabama Train

**Key:** E
**Artist:** Elmore James
**Style:** Slide blues
**Tempo:** Medium shuffle (~100 BPM)

## YouTube
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4crO6moMBto

## Structure
- 12-bar blues × verses
- Slide guitar riff throughout
- Solo section

|-----|------|-----|------|-------|--------|

## Notes
- Elmore James — slide guitar master
- Train imagery is classic blues — "riding the rails"
- Slide guitar in open E or open D tuning
- Driving shuffle feel — mimics a train rhythm
- Good energy number — the train theme gets people moving
- Pair with Dust My Broom for a slide guitar showcase
