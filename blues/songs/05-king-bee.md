# King Bee

**Key:** E
**Artist:** Muddy Waters
**Year:** 1957
**Style:** Chicago blues shuffle
**Tempo:** Medium shuffle (~95 BPM)

## YouTube
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XWLvm11MAaM

## Structure
- 12-bar blues × verses
- Harmonica/guitar solo
- Outro

## Notes
- Muddy Waters at his best
- Playful, suggestive lyrics — crowd loves it
- Shuffle feel — swing those 8th notes
- Great harp number if playing with harmonica player
- Rolling Stones also covered this (Sticky Fingers era)
