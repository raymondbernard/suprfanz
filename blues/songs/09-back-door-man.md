# Back Door Man

**Key:** A7
**Artist:** The Doors (Willie Dixon original)
**Year:** 1961 (Wolf) / 1967 (Doors)
**Style:** Blues-rock
**Tempo:** Medium (~100 BPM)

## YouTube
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=offeVk26Bn4

## Structure
- 12-bar blues × verses
- Guitar/organ solo
- Breakdown section
- Return to verse

## Notes
- Willie Dixon wrote it, Howlin' Wolf recorded it, The Doors made it famous
- The Doors version adds organ, heavier rhythm, Jim Morrison's delivery
- Great crossover — blues fans and rock fans both love it
- Slightly darker, edgier vibe
- The "back door man" is the guy who sneaks out the back
- Good energy builder mid-set
