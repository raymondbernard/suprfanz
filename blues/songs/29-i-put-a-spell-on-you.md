## Notes
- Screamin' Jay Hawkins — the theatrical blues originator
- Minor key — spooky, dramatic, intense
- The vocal is everything — growling, howling, possessed
- Covered by Nina Simone, Creedence, Alan Price
- "I put a spell on you because you're mine" — obsession as love song
- Perfect for showing theatrical side of blues
- Great late-set number — build drama, then release
- Fm is a dark, moody key — perfect for this song
