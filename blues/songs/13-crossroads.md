# Crossroads

**Key:** A
**Artist:** Robert Johnson / Cream
**Year:** 1936 (Johnson) / 1968 (Cream)
**Style:** Delta blues / blues-rock
**Tempo:** Fast shuffle (~140 BPM)

## YouTube
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=t-a2IOKrQHY

## Structure
- 12-bar blues × verses
- Extended guitar solo (Cream version)
- Outro

## Notes
- Robert Johnson wrote it (1936) — Cream made it a rock anthem
- Cream version (Live at Winterland) is the reference for most bands
- Fast, driving shuffle — high energy
- Great set closer or peak-of-set song
- Clapton's soloing on this is legendary
- The "Cross Road Blues" legend — selling soul at the crossroads
- Perfect when the room needs energy injection
