## Notes
- B.B. King classic — "sweet little angel"
- One of B.B.'s most beloved slow blues
- Guitar tone is everything — warm, clean, sustained notes
- Bending and vibrato are the key techniques
- Great for showing sophistication — not just fast playing
- Perfect for senior audiences — slow, romantic, gentle
- C# is a blues key — dark, warm color
- Can feature extended guitar soloing
