# Rollin' and Tumblin'

**Key:** G
**Artist:** Muddy Waters
**Style:** Delta blues, slide guitar
**Tempo:** Medium (~90 BPM)

## YouTube (with measures)
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JQSmGE1Kmnk

## Structure
- 8-bar blues form (not standard 12-bar)
- Slide guitar riff throughout
- Verses over the riff

## Notes
- Delta blues classic — Muddy Waters' first hit (1941)
- Slide guitar in open G tuning (D G D G B D)
- 8-bar form, not 12-bar — shorter cycle
- Raw, hypnotic groove
- Great for intimate settings and senior audiences
- Can feature harp or slide guitar prominently
