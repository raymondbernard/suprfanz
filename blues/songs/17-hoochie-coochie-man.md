# Hoochie Coochie Man

**Key:** A
**Artist:** Muddy Waters / Willie Dixon
**Year:** 1954
**Style:** Chicago blues
**Tempo:** Medium (~85 BPM)

## YouTube
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fQ4NFsw4bOU

## Structure
- Stop-time intro
- 12-bar blues × verses
- Guitar/harp solo
- Return to verse

## Notes
- Willie Dixon wrote it, Muddy Waters made it an anthem
- The "I'm the hoochie coochie man" boasts — pure swagger
- Stop-time figure at the top of each chorus — dramatic pauses
- Great for audience participation — "everybody knows I'm here!"
- One of the most covered blues songs ever
- Perfect mid-set energy boost
- The gypsy woman / mojo theme is classic blues mythology
