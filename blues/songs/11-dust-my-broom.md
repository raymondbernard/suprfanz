# Dust My Broom

**Key:** C
**Artist:** Elmore James
**Year:** 1951
**Style:** Slide blues shuffle
**Tempo:** Medium shuffle (~105 BPM)

## YouTube
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IPwKZSHwg5A

## Structure
- 12-bar blues × verses
- Slide guitar riff is the signature
- Solo section

## Signature Slide Riff
```
C:  C  E  G  Bb C  (slide up to C, riff on top)
```

## Notes
- Elmore James' signature song — the slide riff is iconic
- Open D or open E tuning for slide (D A D F# A D / E A E G# B E)
- One of the most recognizable blues riffs ever
- Shuffle feel — driving rhythm
- Great energy raiser — gets toes tapping
- Covered by everyone from Allman Brothers to John Lee Hooker
