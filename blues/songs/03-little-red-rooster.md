# Little Red Rooster

**Key:** G (12-bar version)
**Artist:** Howlin' Wolf / Rolling Stones
**Year:** 1961 (Wolf) / 1964 (Stones)
**Style:** Slow Chicago blues
**Tempo:** Slow (~70 BPM)

## Notes
- Slow, gritty, Delta-style feel
- Slide guitar fits perfectly
- Vocal delivery is conversational, storytelling
- Rolling Stones had a #1 UK hit with this
- Great for showing dynamics — quiet verses, louder choruses
- **Vocal starts on the 1** — "I'm" hits on beat 1 of bar 1
