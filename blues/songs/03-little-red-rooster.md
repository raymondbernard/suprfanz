# Little Red Rooster

**Key:** G (12-bar version)
**Artist:** Rolling Stones (Willie Dixon)
**Year:** 1964
**Style:** Slow Chicago blues
**Tempo:** Slow (~70 BPM)

## YouTube
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Lr0q0zlrZXM

## Structure
- 12-bar blues form × verses
- Guitar fills between vocal lines
- Solo section

## Chord Progression (12-bar blues)

**Nashville Notation:** I7 — IV7 — I7 — V7 — I7 / V7 (turnaround)

## Notes
- Slow, gritty, Delta-style feel
- Slide guitar fits perfectly
- Vocal delivery is conversational, storytelling
- Rolling Stones version — #1 UK hit in 1964, the only pure blues song to ever top the UK singles chart
- Great for showing dynamics — quiet verses, louder choruses
- **Vocal starts on the 1** — "I'm" hits on beat 1 of bar 1
