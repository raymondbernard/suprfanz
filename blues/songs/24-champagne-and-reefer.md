## Notes
- Muddy Waters — late period, still powerful
- "Champagne and reefer" — celebrating the good life
- Good-time blues — not sad, just grooving
- A7 key gives it a bright, open feel
- Great for lighter moments in the set
- The Rolling Stones covered this with Muddy (1981)
