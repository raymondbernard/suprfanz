**Key:** G

**Year:** 1971
**Style:** Texas blues shuffle
**Tempo:** Medium shuffle (~100 BPM)

## Notes
- Freddie King classic — covered by everyone (Jeff Beck, Gary Moore)
- Written by Don Nix
- Guitar-driven, riff-based blues
- Shuffle feel — swing the 8th notes
- Great vehicle for extended guitar soloing
- Crowd pleaser when played with energy
- **Vocal starts on the 1** — "I'm" hits on beat 1 of bar 1
