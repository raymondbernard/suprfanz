# Bad to the Bone

**Key:** G
**Artist:** George Thorogood & The Destroyers
**Year:** 1982
**Style:** Blues-rock
**Tempo:** Medium slow (~85 BPM)

## YouTube
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8KciRaANKmo

## Structure
- Iconic riff intro
- 12-bar blues × verses
- Guitar solo
- Outro with riff

## Notes
- George Thorogood's signature song — everyone knows it
- The riff is iconic — must nail it perfectly
- "Bad to the bone" — pure attitude and swagger
- Used in every movie/trailer ever (Terminator 2, etc.)
- Perfect set closer — leaves them energized
- Great for any audience — universally recognized
- The riff carries the whole song — simple but unforgettable
- **Vocal starts on the 1** — "Bad" hits on beat 1 of bar 1
