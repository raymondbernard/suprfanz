# SuprFanz Promo Video — New Script

## Concept

A 60–90 second brand video for SuprFanz.com repositioning the product as an **AI agent specifically trained to market live events on social media and email**.

It removes all Neo4j/graph-database messaging and replaces it with a clean, modern narrative: an AI agent that learns your event, finds your fans, writes your messages, and drives attendance.

---

## Tone & Style

- Energetic, modern, blues/rock undercurrent
- Fast cuts, bold type, UI motion, human faces
- Brand colors: Yellow 600 `#fdd835`, Blue 200 `#90caf9`, white
- Font: DIN Next Rounded LT Pro
- Music: driving blues-rock shuffle, building energy

---

## Script

### Scene 1 — Hook (0:00–0:08)

**Visual:** Empty stage. A single spotlight. A mic stand. A poster on a brick wall: “Tonight — Blues Showcase.” A hand slaps a “CANCELLED?” sticker on it. Cut to a phone screen scrolling past dozens of event invites.

**VO (Ray / Cosmic Ray):**
> “You built the show. Booked the room. Posted the event. Now you wait… and hope fans show up.”

**On-screen text:**
> POSTING ISN’T PROMOTING.

---

### Scene 2 — Problem (0:08–0:20)

**Visual:** Montage of an artist at 2 AM copying and pasting the same message into DMs, sending emails, checking RSVP counts. Clock ticking. Coffee cups stacking.

**VO:**
> “Social promo eats 20 hours a week. Messages feel impersonal. Fans miss the invite. And half the room sits empty.”

**On-screen text (staccato):**
- 20 HRS / WEEK
- SAME MESSAGE, 100 TIMES
- EMPTY SEATS

---

### Scene 3 — The Shift (0:20–0:28)

**Visual:** Phone screen zooms into the SuprFanz app. The robot mascot animates. A clean dashboard appears: event connected, fan list loading, message drafts generating.

**VO:**
> “What if you had an AI agent trained specifically to market your event?”

**On-screen text:**
> MEET SUPRFANZ.

---

### Scene 4 — Agent in Action (0:28–0:50)

**Visual:** Split-screen or quick cuts showing the agent working:
1. Paste event URL
2. Agent scans audience and past events
3. Agent drafts personalized messages
4. Agent targets fans by location, history, and engagement
5. Messages go out across Facebook, email, SMS
6. RSVPs roll in

Use actual UI screenshots/dashboard motion if available. Show real names, first-name personalization, “Going / Maybe / Not Replied” targeting.

**VO:**
> “SuprFanz is an AI agent built to fill rooms. It studies your event, finds your SuprFanz, writes messages in your voice, and sends them at the right time — on social media, email, and SMS. It even learns from every campaign, so the next show is easier than the last.”

**On-screen text (synchronized to visuals):**
- CONNECT YOUR EVENT
- FIND YOUR FANS
- DRAFT PERSONAL MESSAGES
- TARGET BY LOCATION & HISTORY
- SEND ACROSS SOCIAL + EMAIL
- SELL OUT

---

### Scene 5 — Proof (0:50–0:58)

**Visual:** Crowded room. People dancing. Stage shot from behind the band. Cosmic Ray performing. Quick shot of sold-out RSVPs on screen.

**VO:**
> “From B.B. King’s blues residency to venues everywhere — SuprFanz turns your fan graph into a full room.”

**On-screen text:**
- 120 SEAT ROOM: SOLD OUT
- 456 FANS REACHED
- 10–20 HOURS SAVED

---

### Scene 6 — CTA (0:58–1:08)

**Visual:** Robot mascot flies in with cape. URL appears: **SuprFanz.com**. “Bring Back SuprFanz” or “Get Early Access” button animates.

**VO:**
> “Everyone deserves SuprFanz. Join the comeback.”

**On-screen text:**
> EVERYONE DESERVES SUPRFANZ
> SuprFanz.com

---

## Editing Notes

- No Neo4j logos, mentions, or graph visualizations.
- No database jargon.
- All analytics/engine references should be framed as “AI agent learns,” not “graph database powers.”
- Keep the original mascot and blue/yellow brand identity.
- If possible, use real SuprFanz dashboard UI and live show footage.
