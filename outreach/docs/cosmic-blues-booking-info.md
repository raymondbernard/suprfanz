# Cosmic Blues Band — Booking Information

## Band Overview
The Cosmic Blues Band is a NYC-based blues band led by **Cosmic Ray** (Ray Bernard), former Resident Band Leader at B.B. King's Blues Club in Times Square for approximately 10 years. The band performs classic blues, roots, and soul music — perfect for senior audiences who grew up with this music.

## Why Book Us for Your Senior Center
- **Music seniors love** — Classic blues from Howlin' Wolf, Muddy Waters, B.B. King, Rolling Stones, Eric Clapton, and more
- **Familiar songs** — Recognizable hits from the 50s, 60s, and 70s that get toes tapping
- **Professional experience** — Former B.B. King's house band, experienced performers
- **Flexible format** — Can do full band performance or acoustic duo
- **Reasonable rates** — Special pricing for NYC senior centers and community programs
- **All five boroughs** — Willing to travel anywhere in NYC

## Performance Options
1. **Full Band (4-5 pieces)** — Guitar, bass, drums, vocals, optional harmonica/keys
2. **Acoustic Duo** — Guitar and vocals, lighter sound, smaller space needed
3. **Blues Trio** — Guitar, bass, drums

## Technical Requirements
- **Full band:** Minimum 10x12 foot stage area, PA system (can provide our own if needed), 4 power outlets
- **Acoustic duo:** Minimal — just a power outlet and seating area
- **Setup time:** 45 minutes for full band, 15 minutes for acoustic duo
- **Performance length:** 60-90 minutes (can be adjusted to your schedule)
