# Cosmic Blues Band — Senior Center Phone Script

## When Calling NYC DFTA Senior Centers

### Introduction
"Hello, my name is Ray Bernard, I'm the band leader of the Cosmic Blues Band. I'm calling because we'd love to perform at your senior center. We play classic blues and roots music — songs from B.B. King, Muddy Waters, the Rolling Stones, that era — music your members will recognize and enjoy.

I was actually the resident band leader at B.B. King's Blues Club in Times Square for about ten years, so we're professional and experienced.

I got your center's information from the NYC Department for the Aging directory."

### If They're Interested
"Great! A few quick questions:
1. Do you have any upcoming events or regular programming where live music would fit?
2. Do you have a space where we could perform — a common room or activity hall?
3. Do you have a sound system, or would we need to bring our own?
4. What days and times work best for your members?

We can do a full band or just an acoustic duo, depending on your space and preferences. We offer special rates for DFTA-funded senior centers."

### If They Need to Check With Someone
"Of course, I understand. Who would be the best person to speak with about booking entertainment? Would that be the center director or the activities coordinator? 

Can I leave my contact information? My name is Ray Bernard, phone number is 929-361-7136, and my email is cosmicraymusic@gmail.com. I can also send over some information about the band and a link to a live performance video."

### If They're Not Interested
"No problem at all, thank you for your time. Have a great day!"

### If They Want Email Info
"Perfect, I'll send over an email with our booking information, a sample setlist, and a link to our live video. You can reach me at cosmicraymusic@gmail.com or 929-361-7136 if you have any questions."

### Key Points to Remember
- **Be warm and friendly** — these are community centers, not clubs
- **Mention B.B. King's** — instant credibility
- **Emphasize the music** — classic blues, familiar songs, music seniors grew up with
- **Be flexible** — offer acoustic duo as a low-pressure option
- **Ask about their programming** — many centers have regular entertainment budgets
- **Follow up** — if they say maybe, call back in a week

### Call Tracking
Update the CSV after each call:
- Status: CONTACTED, INTERESTED, BOOKED, NOT_INTERESTED, NO_ANSWER
- Date contacted

- Notes from the call

### Best Times to Call
- **Tuesday-Thursday, 10:00 AM - 2:00 PM** — center is open, staff are available
- **Avoid Mondays** — busy start of week
- **Avoid Fridays after 1 PM** — winding down for weekend
