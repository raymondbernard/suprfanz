# Senior Centers & Assisted Living Campaign

## Overview
Separate outreach campaign targeting senior centers, assisted living facilities, nursing homes, and older adult centers in the NYC area. These facilities book live entertainment regularly for their residents and are a great fit for Cosmic Blues Band's acoustic/blues repertoire.

## Why Senior Facilities?
- **Regular bookings** — facilities book entertainment weekly or monthly
- **Steady income** — many have dedicated activities budgets
- **Appreciative audience** — seniors love live blues, jazz, and roots music
- **Daytime hours** — most performances are afternoon/early evening (great for filling calendar gaps)
- **Repeat bookings** — facilities re-book acts that perform well
- **Low competition** — fewer bands target this market

## Target Facilities
- **Assisted Living** (12 facilities) — Sunrise, Atria, Harbour View, Juniper, CareOne, Brighton Gardens, Mira Vie, The Veranda, The Brielle, Waterford, Brooklyn Adult Care
- **Senior Centers** (12 centers) — Encore, VISIONS, SAGE, Queens Center for Gay Seniors, Lenox Hill, Hamilton Madison, City Hall, Ozanam, Peter Dellamonica, St. Charles, Northeastern Queens, Sunset Park, Riverstone, Grand Street

## Total: 34 facilities/centers across NYC + nearby NJ

## Booking Approach
1. **Contact role**: Activities Director / Program Director (they book all entertainment)
2. **Phone first** — call to get the activities director's name and email, then follow up
3. **Email follow-up** — send EPK + press release + sample setlist
4. **Offer demo** — offer a free 30-min demo performance
5. **Pricing** — $150-300 per performance (typical rate for senior facilities)
6. **Setlist** — blues classics, jazz standards, soul, familiar tunes from 50s-70s + original blues

## Key Platforms for Getting Booked
- **Play it for Seniors** (playitforseniors.org) — marketplace connecting entertainers to senior facilities
- **GigSalad** (gigsalad.com) — book gigs at senior communities
- **Entertainers for Seniors** (entertainersforseniors.com) — booking resource
- **Seniors Love Live Music** (seniorslovelivemusic.com) — specializes in senior living entertainment
- **Good Time Seniors** (goodtimeseniors.com) — non-profit connecting musicians to senior centers
