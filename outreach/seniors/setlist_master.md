## Set Flow Tips
- Avoid too many songs in the same key back-to-back
- Open with energy: Green Onions (F) or Boom Boom (E)
- Slow it down mid-set: Stormy Monday (G) or The Thrill Is Gone (Gb)
- Close with power: Bad to the Bone (G) or Sunshine of Your Love (C)
- For senior sets: lean on Stormy Monday, The Thrill Is Gone, Sweet Little Angel, Rock Me Baby
