## 1. The Product — What We’re Actually Selling

**"Silvana Blues Review"**
- A curated **4-band blues showcase** in one night.
- Each band plays a tight **30-40 minute set**.
- **Cosmic Blues Band closes the night** as the anchor/house band (45-50 min set).
- Stripped-down, real-deal electric blues — no backing tracks, no pop covers.
- Silvana’s downstairs speakeasy room (~100 capacity) with full backline + professional sound.
- Middle Eastern cafe/restaurant upstairs = full evening destination: dinner, drinks, music.

**Why This Works for Silvana:**
- They already book blues/jazz (Phil Young House Of Blues).
- Same ownership team as Shrine World Music Venue = experienced bookers, cross-promotion potential.
- Restaurant format means people come early for hummus/falafel/shawarma and stay for the show.
- Harlem has no dedicated monthly blues review on the 116th St corridor.
- Full backline = fast changeovers, lower band load-in stress.

| 11:00 PM | Room winds down | — | Last call, merch, mailing list signup |
| 12:00 AM | Close | — | |

### Band Booking Strategy
**Goal:** Book 4 bands total each month, with Cosmic Blues Band as the consistent anchor.

**Band 1 (Opener) — Local/Student-Friendly:**
- A young or up-and-coming blues act.
- Columbia student band, if available (Postcrypt connection).
- Low fee or door-only split.
- Draw: 10-25 people.

**Band 2 — Chicago/Delta Traditional:**
- Authentic blues trio or quartet.
- Brings musicians and purists.
- Fee: $75-150 or tip split (we run free admission).

**Band 3 — Blues-Rock / Electric:**
- Slightly heavier, younger crowd draw.
- Bands like The Hitman Blues Band, Spiros Soukis, Spring Brooks.
- Fee: $100-200 or tip split (we run free admission).

**Band 4 — The Cosmic Blues Band (Anchor):**
- Consistent closer every month.
- Longest set, biggest draw.
- Builds brand recognition: "Cosmic Blues Band hosts Silvana Blues Review."

## 6. Pricing & Revenue Model

### Cover Charge Options
| Option | Price | Use Case |
|---|---|---|
| Free admission | $0 | Always available at door |
| Tip jar / Band tips | $ suggested | At the show |
| Band tips | $ suggested | Standard support |
| Dinner + Show | Food purchase | Bundle with Silvana food menu |
| Free all night | $0 | Dine-in or drink customers welcome |

### Revenue Split (Propose to Silvana)

**Option A — Free Admission + Tips (Recommended for launch):**
- Admission is free all night.
- Silvana keeps 100% of food/bar sales.
- A tip jar (cash + QR code) is split evenly among the 4 bands.
- Strong attendance drives bigger tips and proves the series to Silvana.

**Revenue model example:**
- 80 attendees, average $5 tip each = $400 total tips
- $100 per band for the night
- Silvana also benefits from food/bar sales across the full evening.

**Option B — Food/Bar Minimum Guarantee:**
- Silvana guarantees $400 in food/bar sales for the night.
- If we hit it, Silvana extends the residency.
- Tips remain with bands.
- Low risk for both sides.

**Option C — Tips + Small House Fee:**
- Silvana pays $100 house fee/sound to Cosmic Blues Band.
- All tips split among bands.
- Useful once the series is proven and we manage production.

### Month 1 Financial Goal
- **Attendance target:** 80-120
- **Admission revenue:** $0 (free)
- **Tips estimate:** $200-400 total ($50-100 per band)
- **Silvana food/bar uplift:** primary venue benefit
- **Cosmic Blues Band net:** equal tip split month 1; long-term, negotiate small production fee if we book/market the whole series.
