# How to Submit a Press Release to Blues Media

## Summary

There are **three main ways** to submit press releases to blues media outlets:

1. **Email** — most common. Send press release + EPK link directly to the editor/contact email.
2. **Physical CD** — some magazines (Blues Blast, Living Blues) require physical CDs for album reviews.
3. **Web form** — some outlets have contact forms on their websites.

## What to Send

### For a Press Release (email)
1. **Subject line:** "New NYC blues from former B.B. King's bandleader — EPK available"
2. **Body:** Personalized pitch (see templates in `press_release.md`)
3. **Attachments:** EPK (PDF), 1-2 high-res photos
4. **Links:** YouTube live clips, social media

### For an Album Review (physical)
1. **CD** (2 copies for Living Blues)
2. **One-sheet** — brief album description, track list, release date
3. **Bio** — artist background
4. **Contact info** — email + phone

### For Radio Airplay
1. **MP3** (email) or **CD** (physical)
2. **One-sheet** with track listing
3. **Contact info**
4. **Note:** mention you're available for in-studio interview

## Best Practices

1. **Personalize each email** — use the contact's name, reference their outlet
2. **Keep it short** — 3-4 paragraphs max
3. **Lead with the hook** — "former B.B. King's bandleader" is your strongest angle
4. **Include video links** — live clips are more compelling than text
5. **Follow up once** — 1 week after initial send, short "just checking in"
6. **Don't spam** — one email per outlet, follow up once
7. **Time it right** — send Tuesday-Thursday, 9-11 AM their timezone
8. **For reviews:** send physical CDs — they get more attention than digital
