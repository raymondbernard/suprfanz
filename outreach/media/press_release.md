### FOR THE BLUES THAT WON'T QUIT: COSMIC RAY RETURNS WITH THE COSMIC BLUES BAND — RESIDENCY-BRED NYC BLUES THAT SCALES FROM SOLO TO FULL BAND

**NEW YORK, NY —** The man who kept the lights on at B.B. King's Blues Club for nearly a decade is back. Cosmic Ray — former Resident Band Leader and Promoter at the legendary Times Square venue — returns with The Cosmic Blues Band, bringing raw, high-energy cosmic blues to rooms across New York and beyond.

After B.B. King's closed its doors, the project went quiet. Now it's back, and it's built for flexibility: solo guitar and vocals for intimate listening rooms, a duo or trio for club dates, or the full four-piece for rooms that can take the noise. Whatever the configuration, the sound stays raw, groove-heavy, and unmistakably cosmic.

"I spent almost ten years booking the talent, hosting the nights, and leading the house band at B.B. King's," says Cosmic Ray. "We packed Lucille's so much they moved us to the main room. That energy doesn't go away — it just finds a new room."

The Cosmic Blues Band draws on a rotating pool of seasoned NYC players, all self-contained on backline. Fresh off a residency at Toronto's Gladstone Hotel, the band is booking select dates for fall and winter 2026.
