### Design Notes
- **Colors:** Dark background (black or deep blue), gold/white text — blues club aesthetic
- **Photo:** Live shot of Cosmic Ray on guitar, or Silvana's exterior at night
- **Font:** Bold, readable from 10 feet (people walking by)
- **QR code:** Link to Instagram @cosmicbluesband or Silvana calendar
- **Size:** 8.5x11 (standard) + 5x7 (smaller for bulletin boards)

## Where to Post Flyers

### Primary (must do)
1. **Silvana bulletin board** — inside the venue
2. **Shrine bulletin board** — inside the venue
3. **Silvana front window** (if allowed)

### 116th Street Corridor
4. **Corner stores / bodegas** along 116th St between Frederick Douglass and Manhattan Ave
5. **Cafes near 116th St** (Silvana's neighbors)
6. **Laundromats** on 116th St
7. **Barbershops/salons** on 116th St and Frederick Douglass Blvd

### Nearby Music/Culture
8. **Apollo Theater area** (125th St)
9. **Minton's** (Harlem jazz club, 118th St)
10. **Lenox Saphire** (Harlem bar)
11. **Bill's Place** (Harlem blues venue, 133rd St)
12. **Shrine area businesses** (Frederick Douglass Blvd corridor)

### Transit
13. **116th St subway station** (B/C) — community board area
14. **125th St station** (A/B/C/D) — if allowed
