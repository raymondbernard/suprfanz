# Weekly Checklist — Silvana Residency

## Every Week

### 📅 2 Days Before Show
- [ ] Post Instagram Story: "Cosmic Blues at Silvana in 2 days"
- [ ] Post Facebook Event reminder (if using FB Events)
- [ ] Send weekly email to mailing list
- [ ] Post in Shrine NYC Facebook Group: "This [DAY] at Silvana"
- [ ] Post in NYC Blues Facebook Group

### 📅 Day Before Show
- [ ] Post Instagram Story: "Tomorrow at Silvana"
- [ ] Send WhatsApp/Signal group message: "Who's coming tomorrow?"
- [ ] Check doNYC listing is live

### 📅 Day of Show
- [ ] Post Instagram Story in the morning: "Tonight at Silvana"
- [ ] Post Facebook: "Tonight — Cosmic Blues at Silvana, Harlem"
- [ ] Tweet: "Tonight: Cosmic Blues at Silvana. 300 W. 116th St. Come through."
- [ ] Arrive early — set up, sound check
- [ ] Put mailing list clipboard + pen at the door
- [ ] Put Residency Regular cards on the table
- [ ] Make sure QR code is visible

### 📅 During Show
- [ ] Record 2-3 clips (30 sec each) for Instagram Stories
- [ ] Take 1-2 good photos
- [ ] Mention the next show from the stage
- [ ] Invite people to join the mailing list + WhatsApp group
- [ ] Hand out Regular Cards to people who stay

### 📅 Day After Show
- [ ] Post Instagram: best clip + photo from last night
- [ ] Post Instagram Story: "Last night at Silvana" with clips
- [ ] Post Facebook: "Thanks to everyone who came through. We do it again [DAY]"
- [ ] Add new mailing list signups to the list
- [ ] Send WhatsApp/Signal group: "Thanks for coming! Next week [DAY]"
- [ ] Update fbfriends.csv with anyone who mentioned they came

### 📅 Weekly Stats to Track
- [ ] How many people came (rough count)
- [ ] How many new mailing list signups
- [ ] How many Regular Cards handed out
- [ ] How many Instagram followers gained
- [ ] Any special guests / sit-ins

---

## Monthly

### First [DAY] of the Month
- [ ] Update doNYC listing with the month's dates
- [ ] Update Bandsintown with the month's dates
- [ ] Create Facebook Events for the month's shows
- [ ] Post monthly newsletter to email list
- [ ] Post monthly Instagram: "[Month] at Silvana — here's what's coming"

### Last [DAY] of the Month: Blues Jam Night
- [ ] Promote jam night 1 week ahead in Facebook Groups
- [ ] Post: "Blues Jam at Silvana this [DAY] — bring your instrument"
- [ ] Bring sign-up sheet for musicians
- [ ] Take photos/video of the jam for socials
- [ ] Tag any musicians who sit in

---

## Quarterly

### Every 3 Months
- [ ] Review: How's attendance? Growing? Stable? Declining?
- [ ] Review: Which marketing channels are working? (FB, Instagram, flyers, word of mouth)
- [ ] Update flyer design (keep it fresh)
- [ ] Reach out to press for a follow-up story (3-month residency milestone)
- [ ] Consider adding a second night if demand is there
- [ ] Plan a special event (anniversary show, guest night, live recording)
