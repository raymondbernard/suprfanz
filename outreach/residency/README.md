## Campaign Overview

### Phase 1: Pre-Launch (2 weeks before first show)
- Post in Shrine Facebook Group
- Instagram collab with @silvanaharlem
- Email Harlem press (Amsterdam News, Harlem World, etc.)
- Flyer Silvana, Shrine, and 116th St corridor
- List on doNYC + Bandsintown
- FB Messenger campaign to NY friends

### Phase 2: Launch Week
- FB Event for first night
- Instagram Stories countdown
- Cross-promote with other Silvana/Shrine acts
- Email reminder to mailing list (if any)

### Phase 3: Ongoing (every week)
- Instagram Stories every residency night
- Post clips next day
- FB Event for each night
- Mailing list signup at the door
- "Residency Regular" loyalty cards
- Monthly email to list with upcoming dates

### Phase 4: Community Building
- Blues jam night (monthly)
- Special guest sit-ins
- "Shawarma + Blues" promotion
- WhatsApp/Signal group for regulars
