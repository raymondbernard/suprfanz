## Campaign Goal

Market the Cosmic Blues Band residency at Silvana to people who would attend — Harlem locals, blues/jazz fans, Shrine regulars, and NYC music lovers.

## Target Audiences

1. **Shrine/Silvana regulars** — people who follow @silvanaharlem and @shrineharlem on Instagram/Facebook
2. **Harlem music scene** — locals who attend live music in Harlem
3. **NYC blues fans** — our existing Facebook friends who live in NY (already in fbfriends.csv)
4. **Jazz/world music fans** — Silvana's existing audience
5. **Music press** — media who cover Harlem nightlife, live music, blues

## Campaign Components

### 1. Facebook Messenger Outreach
- Target: NY-area Facebook friends (already have 1309 pending in fbfriends.csv)
- Message: Announce residency at Silvana, invite to come check it out
- Different event URL or direct invite (not the Facebook Event — a personal invite to the residency)

### 2. Social Media
- Post on @cosmicbluesband Instagram
- Share to Shrine NYC Facebook Group
- Share to NYC Blues Facebook Groups
- Tag @silvanaharlem and @shrineharlem

### 3. Email Newsletter
- Send to any email contacts we have
- Announce: "Cosmic Blues Band residency at Silvana — every [DAY] night in Harlem"

### 4. Flyer/Poster
- Design for Silvana's bulletin board
- Harlem neighborhood flyering

### 5. Press
- Pitch to NYC music press: "New blues residency at Silvana Harlem"
- Harlem-specific outlets: Harlem One Stop, Harlem World Magazine, etc.
