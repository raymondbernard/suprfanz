### FROM B.B. KING'S TO HARLEM: COSMIC RAY LAUNCHES RESIDENCY AT SILVANA — SISTER TO LEGENDARY SHRINE VENUE

**HARLEM, NY —** The man who kept the blues alive at B.B. King's Blues Club in Times Square for nearly a decade is bringing that fire to Harlem.

Cosmic Ray — former Resident Band Leader and Promoter at the legendary B.B. King's — has launched a weekly residency at **Silvana** (300 W. 116th Street), the sister venue to Harlem's iconic **Shrine World Music Venue**. Both venues, owned by Abdel Ouedraogo, have been cornerstones of Harlem's live music scene since 2007 and 2013 respectively.

"After B.B. King's closed, I needed to find the right room — a room with soul, with history, with a community that loves live music," says Cosmic Ray. "Silvana and Shrine have been doing exactly that in Harlem for years. It feels right."

The residency features The Cosmic Blues Band in flexible configurations — solo guitar and vocals for intimate nights, duo or trio for club sets, and the full four-piece when the room demands it. The sound draws on Cosmic Ray's decade at B.B. King's: raw, groove-heavy cosmic blues that scales to the room.

Silvana, known for its Middle Eastern cuisine, boutique, and nightly live music, offers a unique setting — a Harlem basement club with the kind of intimacy that Times Square lost. The residency continues a tradition of live blues in Harlem that stretches back to the Apollo Theater and beyond.

**Key facts:**
- Former Resident Band Leader & Promoter, B.B. King's Blues Club, Times Square (~10 years)
- New residency at Silvana, sister to Shrine World Music Venue (founded 2007)
- Flexible lineup: solo → duo → trio → full band
- Silvana: 300 W. 116th Street, Harlem NYC
- No cover charge. Full bar + Middle Eastern food menu.
