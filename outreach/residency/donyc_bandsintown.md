# doNYC + Bandsintown Listings

## doNYC (free events calendar)

### How to List

1. Go to https://donyc.com/
2. Click "Add Event" (or sign in if you have an account)
3. Create a recurring event:

**Event details:**
```
Event Name: Cosmic Blues Band Residency at Silvana
Venue: Silvana (search for it — it's already in their system)
Address: 300 W. 116th Street, New York, NY 10026
Date: Every [DAY] night
Time: [START TIME]
Cover: No cover
Age: 21+ (or all ages — check with Silvana)
Description: Former B.B. King's house bandleader Cosmic Ray brings The Cosmic Blues Band to Silvana Harlem. Solo to full band. Raw, high-energy cosmic blues. No cover. Middle Eastern food. Full bar. Sister to Shrine World Music Venue.
Tags: Blues, Live Music, Jazz, Harlem
```

4. Set as recurring (weekly)
5. Add a photo (live shot of the band)
6. Submit

### Why doNYC Matters
- Silvana is already listed on doNYC
- Shrine is already listed on doNYC
- People search doNYC for "things to do tonight in Harlem"
- Free to list, high visibility

## Bandsintown

### How to List

1. Create an artist account at https://artists.bandsintown.com/
2. Claim your artist page (search for "Cosmic Blues Band" or create new)
3. Add each residency show as a recurring event:

**Show details:**
```
Artist: Cosmic Blues Band
Venue: Silvana
Address: 300 W. 116th Street, New York, NY 10026
Date: Every [DAY] night
Time: [START TIME]
Ticket: Free / No cover
Description: Cosmic Blues Band residency at Silvana Harlem. Sister to Shrine. Former B.B. King's bandleader. Solo to full band.
```

4. Set as recurring weekly event
5. Add a photo
6. Connect your Instagram/Spotify to auto-sync

### Why Bandsintown Matters
- Fans who follow you get notified automatically
- Silvana is already on Bandsintown (venue ID 10003103)
- Shows up in "events near you" searches
- Free to use

## Other Free Listing Sites

### Eventbrite
- Create a free recurring event
- Use as landing page for FB Messenger campaign
- People can "register" (free) and get reminders

### Facebook Events
- Create a recurring FB Event
- Invite NY friends (use the same fbfriends.csv)
- Share to NYC Blues Facebook Groups
- Pin to your page

### Songkick
- Claim artist page at https://www.songkick.com/artist-claim
- List residency shows
- Fans get notified

### Google Events
- Create event in Google Calendar
- Share link in FB Messenger campaign
- Shows up in Google search for "blues harlem tonight"
