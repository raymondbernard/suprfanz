# Cosmic Blues Band — Festival Booking Information

## Band Overview
The Cosmic Blues Band is a NYC-based blues band led by **Cosmic Ray** (Ray Bernard), former Resident Band Leader at B.B. King's Blues Club in Times Square for approximately 10 years. The band performs classic blues, roots, and soul music — the kind of raw, authentic blues that festival audiences crave.

## Why Book Us for Your Blues Festival
- **Authentic blues pedigree** — Former B.B. King's house band leader, 10+ years in the heart of Times Square
- **Festival-ready repertoire** — High-energy blues that gets crowds on their feet: Chicago blues, Delta blues, British blues, and roots rock
- **Professional and reliable** — Experienced performers who show up on time, sound great, and deliver every set
- **Flexible lineup** — Full 4-5 piece band, stripped-down power trio, or acoustic duo depending on your stage and budget
- **Crowd-pleasing sets** — Mix of blues classics and deep cuts that satisfy both casual fans and blues purists
- **Original material available** — Can blend originals with covers for a unique festival set
- **Touring capable** — Available for festivals across the US and internationally

## Performance Options
1. **Full Band (4-5 pieces)** — Guitar, bass, drums, vocals, optional harmonica/keys — best for main stage sets
2. **Power Trio** — Guitar/vocals, bass, drums — tight, punchy, festival-ready
3. **Acoustic Duo** — Guitar and vocals, perfect for workshop stages, side stages, or intimate festival settings

## Technical Requirements
- **Full band:** Minimum 10x12 foot stage area, PA system (can provide our own if needed), 4 power outlets
- **Power trio:** 8x10 foot stage, 3 power outlets, PA (can bring our own)
- **Acoustic duo:** Minimal — just a power outlet and seating area
- **Setup time:** 45 minutes for full band, 20 minutes for trio, 15 minutes for acoustic duo
- **Performance length:** 30-90 minute sets (can be adjusted to your festival schedule)
- **Backline:** Can use provided backline or bring our own — flexible to festival requirements
