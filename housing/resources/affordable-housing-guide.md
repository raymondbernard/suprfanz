# NYC Affordable Housing Resources

## What is Affordable Housing?
Housing where rent is set based on Area Median Income (AMI) — typically 30%, 40%, 50%, 60%, 80% of AMI. Lower AMI = lower rent. Designed for low- and moderate-income New Yorkers.

## Main Pathways to Affordable Housing

### 1. NYC Housing Connect (HPD Lotteries)
- **Website:** https://housingconnect.nyc.gov
- **What:** Apply for affordable rental and homeownership units via lottery
- **Who:** Based on income eligibility per listing
- **How:** Create account, search listings, apply online
- **Tip:** Apply to everything you qualify for — it's a lottery, more applications = more chances
- **Email updates:** Sign up at housingconnect.nyc.gov for new listing alerts
- **Hotline:** 212-863-7990

### 2. NYCHA Public Housing
- **Website:** https://selfserve.nycha.info
- **What:** Public housing developments operated by NYC Housing Authority
- **Who:** Low-income families, elderly, disabled
- **How:** Apply online at selfserve.nycha.info
- **Phone:** 718-707-7771 (Customer Contact Center)
- **Priority codes:**
  - N0: City agency referrals (homeless, fire displacement)
  - N1: Victims of domestic violence
  - N4: Substandard housing, rent burden >50%
  - W0-W3: Working families by income tier
  - W3: 0-30% AMI (lowest income — best chance)

### 3. Section 8 Housing Choice Voucher
- **What:** Federal rent subsidy — tenant pays 30% of income, voucher covers rest
- **Status:** NYCHA waitlist CLOSED (reopened briefly June 2024)
- **Check status:** selfserve.nycha.info
- **HPD Section 8:** Separate program through HPD — also currently closed
- **Tip:** Check periodically — waitlists reopen every few years

### 4. Mitchell-Lama Program
- **What:** Affordable housing for moderate/middle-income families
- **Website:** a806-housingconnect.nyc.gov/nyclottery/lottery.html#ml-home
- **Who:** Income limits vary by building
- **How:** Apply through NYC Mitchell-Lama Connect portal
- **Note:** Many waiting lists are long but some open periodically

### Ready to Rent Program
Free one-on-one financial counseling and application assistance:
- **Website:** nyc.gov/site/hpd/services-and-information/ready-to-rent.page

## Eviction Prevention & Housing Stability

### Homebase
Homelessness prevention services for those at risk of eviction:
- Eviction prevention
- Emergency rental assistance
- Help obtaining public benefits
- Financial counseling
- **Website:** nyc.gov/homebase
- **Phone:** 311

### Rental Supplement Programs
- **LINC:** Moves families from shelter to permanent housing
- **CITYFEPS:** Rent supplement for families with children
- **SEPS:** Rent supplement for individual adults/families without children
- **Pathway Home:** Move from shelter to living with family/friends

## Tips for Low/No Income Applicants
1. **Apply for everything** — Housing Connect, NYCHA, Mitchell-Lama, Section 8 (when open)
2. **Get a referral** — If you're homeless or at risk, getting an N0/N1/W0 priority code dramatically speeds up NYCHA
3. **Use Housing Ambassadors** — They help for free and know the system
4. **Keep your info updated** — NYCHA applications expire after 2 years if not renewed
5. **Check Housing Connect daily** — New lotteries open frequently
6. **Apply at lowest AMI tier** — If you qualify for 30% AMI units, your odds are better (fewer applicants)
7. **Gather documents early** — Income proof, ID, lease/current housing status, family composition
8. **Call 311** — They can connect you to any housing program in NYC
