# ACTIVE LOTTERIES — Apply Now (Manhattan + Williamsburg/Bushwick)
*Updated July 18, 2026*

## HOW TO APPLY
1. Go to **https://housingconnect.nyc.gov**
2. Create an account (or log in if you have one)
3. Search for the lottery by name or ID
4. Fill out the application — it takes 15-30 minutes per listing
5. You can apply to MULTIPLE lotteries at the same time

## ELIGIBILITY — Do You Qualify?
- Must be 18+ years old
- Household income must fall within the listed range for the unit
- Must meet asset limits (currently $200,000 for most units)
- Income = all sources: wages, benefits, SSI, pensions, etc.
- **Zero income is OK** for 30% AMI units — you'll need a zero-income affidavit

## DOCUMENTS TO GATHER NOW

### For Everyone:
- [ ] Photo ID (driver's license, state ID, passport)
- [ ] Social Security card (or number)
- [ ] Birth certificate
- [ ] Current lease or residency proof (utility bill, mail)
- [ ] Bank statements (last 6 months) — all accounts
- [ ] Proof of current income (last 6 pay stubs, or benefit award letters)

### For Zero-Income Applicants:
- [ ] Zero-Income Affidavit (notarized statement declaring no income)
- [ ] Benefit award letters (SNAP, Medicaid, SSI, SSD, cash assistance)
- [ ] Letter from anyone providing support (family, friend, shelter)
- [ ] DHS shelter letter (if in shelter system)
- [ ] HASA letter (if HIV/AIDS client)

### For Self-Employed:
- [ ] Most recent tax return (Schedule C)
- [ ] 6 months of bank statements showing business income

### Asset Documentation:
- [ ] All bank account statements (checking, savings)
- [ ] Investment account statements
- [ ] Property deeds (if any)
- [ ] Vehicle registration (if any)
- [ ] **Asset limit:** $200,000 for most lotteries

## APPLICATION STRATEGY

1. **Apply to ALL 7 low-income active lotteries** above — more applications = more chances
2. **Apply to Tenant Selection waitlists too** — 113 buildings in your target area have open waitlists
3. **If your income is below $34,050** (1 person): You qualify for 30% AMI units — these have the fewest applicants and best odds. Currently no active 30% AMI lotteries in your target area, but check Housing Connect weekly for new ones
4. **If you have zero income**: Apply for NYCHA public housing (selfserve.nycha.info), contact Homebase (311), and apply through SPOA or HASA if applicable
5. **Use Community Board preference**: If you live in the same community board as the building, you get preference for 20-50% of units
6. **Check Housing Connect weekly** — new lotteries open all the time
7. **Sign up for email alerts**: https://lb.benchmarkemail.com//listbuilder/signupnew
